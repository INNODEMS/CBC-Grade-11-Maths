# Figure request — reflection in a line
 
## Objective
Show how a triangle is reflected in a mirror line. Triangle ABC is reflected in
line m to give the image triangle A'B'C'. As students drag the vertices of ABC, or
drag the two points that define the mirror line, the image updates instantly so
they can see the rule in action: the image is the same size and shape as the
original (just flipped), and each corner sits directly opposite its image across
the line — the same distance from the mirror, on the other side.
 
Topic: transformations (reflection)
 
## What moves, what stays fixed
- Draggable: A, B, C (triangle vertices); M1, M2 (two points that define mirror line m)
- Derived: A', B', C' = reflections of A, B, C in m
## Readouts
- Show the distance from A to the mirror line, and from A' to the mirror line, so
  students can see the two are always equal.
